Day 48 – The Verdict Engine
Overview

The Verdict Engine is an AI-powered decision-support application that helps users compare real-world options using grounded, verifiable information instead of fabricated data.

Unlike traditional comparison tools, every claim is supported by real, named sources, allowing users to inspect the evidence behind each score. Users can customize the importance of different decision criteria through interactive weight sliders, instantly updating rankings to reflect their own priorities.

The project emphasizes transparency, explainability, and user control, making AI-assisted decision-making more trustworthy.

What I Learned

Building The Verdict Engine demonstrated that trustworthy AI is about more than producing answers—it requires showing the evidence behind those answers.

Some of the biggest takeaways include:

AI decisions become significantly more reliable when grounded in real, citable sources.
Adjustable weighting allows different users to reach personalized outcomes using the same underlying evidence.
Transparency improves user trust by exposing supporting sources, confidence levels, and assumptions.
Explaining how conflicting information was resolved is just as important as presenting the final recommendation.
Interactive decision-support interfaces make complex comparisons easier to understand and explore.
