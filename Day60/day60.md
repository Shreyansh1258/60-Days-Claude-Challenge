## Overview

Defend Your Experience AI is an intelligent interview preparation platform that helps candidates validate and confidently explain the experiences listed on their resumes. Instead of generating generic interview questions, the application analyzes projects, skills, internships, and achievements to create realistic follow-up questions that challenge users to defend their work like they would in a real technical interview.

The platform identifies weak explanations, highlights confidence gaps, and provides actionable feedback to improve interview performance.

---

## Problem Statement

Many candidates can write impressive resumes but struggle to explain the details of their work during interviews. Recruiters often ask deep follow-up questions that reveal whether a candidate truly understands their projects.

Defend Your Experience AI bridges this gap by transforming every resume into a personalized mock interview.
